Three plans, priced in US dollars with no VAT added:

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Computers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $5 a month | $48 a year | 1 |
| Personal | $10 a month | $96 a year | up to 3 |
| Power | $20 a month | $192 a year | up to 10 |

Annual billing is 20% off and is pre-selected.

- **Computers:** each computer you index with Locus uses one API key. On every plan you can connect
  as many AI apps on that computer as you like.
- **Monthly allowance:** each plan includes a monthly allowance for embedding your documents
  (deeper on the higher plans). Search keeps working after it is used. Managed embedding also has a
  daily limit that resets at 00:00 UTC. The dashboard shows exactly where you stand, and your AI
  app can show it too through the `locus_account_status` tool.
- **No plan needed for local use:** the engine with the `local` embedder is free and needs no
  account.

**Status when this version of Locus was released:** paid plans were not open yet. Checkout was
closed, nobody was charged, and a free trial needed no card. The pricing page always has the
current status. Team and organization plans: see "Teams and organizations".
