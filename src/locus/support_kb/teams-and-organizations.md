Each person in a team runs their own Locus on their own computer and indexes the folders they can
already read. There is no pooled, central index of everyone's files.

What organizations have in the dashboard: owner, admin and member roles, email invites that expire,
an audit log, and admin revocation of a member's Locus API keys.

If you join an organization, its members can see your email, your role, when you joined, whether
you are still a member, when you last used Locus's hosted features and which Locus version you ran.
Its admins can also see its invites and audit log, and can revoke your Locus API keys. Nobody in the
organization can see your files or searches, because Locus doesn't store them.

Team billing: when this version of Locus was released, team plans were not on sale yet. For team or
company pricing, a pilot, or security questions, use the form or contact details on the enterprise
page.
