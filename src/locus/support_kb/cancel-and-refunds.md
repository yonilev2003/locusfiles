- **Cancel:** online at any time from your dashboard, or by email from your account's address.
  Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
- **Refunds:** no refunds, except where the law requires one. This covers the first charge,
  renewals, top-ups and the unused part of a period after you cancel. Where mandatory consumer law
  where you live gives you more, the law wins.
- **EU, EEA and UK consumers** have a right of withdrawal within 14 days of buying, and **consumers
  in Israel** have their cancellation rights under the Consumer Protection Law. The refunds page has
  the exact terms and every way to give notice.

When this version of Locus was released, billing was not live yet, so nobody had been charged. The
refunds page is the policy that applies.

Cancelling a plan does not affect the local engine: it keeps working with `LOCUS_EMBEDDER=local`.
