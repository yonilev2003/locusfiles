Locus is licensed under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0: you may use it for personal, educational, charitable and public-body purposes, and not for work at a company, paid client work, or inside a product or service. Commercial use needs a separate paid licence that cannot be bought yet.

- **Allowed:** personal use (research, study, hobby projects, private use with no commercial
  application in mind), and use by charitable organisations, educational institutions, public
  research organisations, public safety or health organisations, environmental protection
  organisations and government institutions.
- **Not allowed without a separate licence:** work for a company, paid client work, or use inside a
  product or service, including when you are one person.
- **Can I buy a commercial licence?** Not yet: none exists. Leave your details at
  https://locusfiles.com/commercial and you will be told when one does.
- **Is it open source?** No. It is source-available. Versions of Locus before 0.2.0 were released
  under a different licence (FSL-1.1-ALv2) and stay under it.
- **Does Locus check?** No. There is no licence key and no lock. On the first run of `locus setup`
  or `locus doctor` in a terminal it asks one question and keeps the answer on your computer; it sends
  nothing. `locus license` prints this summary.

This is a plain-words summary, not legal advice. The binding text is the LICENSE file that ships with
Locus. If it is not clear whether your use is commercial, treat it as commercial and ask on the page
above.
